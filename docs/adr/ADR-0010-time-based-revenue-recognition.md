# ADR-0010: Time-Based Revenue Recognition Schedules

**Feature:** Support straight-line daily deferral and recognition for posted invoice revenue.

**Why chosen:**

- The accounting application needs a controlled way to move unearned posted revenue to a liability and recognize it over a defined service period.
- Recognition entries are generated by a service with explicit dates, account checks, and idempotent progress tracking.
- The feature is intentionally limited to a schedule type that can be expressed as an amount over an inclusive date range.

**Strengths:**

- Deferral and recognition use balanced journal entries and are auditable.
- Recognition can be run through a selected date and safely retried without duplicate postings.
- Schedule and account ownership are validated against the current business.

**Alternatives considered:**

- Automatically defer every invoice — rejected because not all invoices represent obligations satisfied over time.
- Implement a complete IFRS 15/ASC 606 engine — rejected for this change; it requires contract, performance-obligation, transaction-price, and allocation workflows beyond a simple schedule.
- Use monthly-only recognition — rejected because date-bounded daily recognition handles partial periods without imposing arbitrary month-end behavior.

**Limitations:**

This schedule does not determine whether a contract qualifies for deferral or whether recognition is over time or at a point in time. It is not a substitute for jurisdictional standards analysis or professional accounting judgment.
